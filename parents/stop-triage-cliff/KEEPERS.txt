Object (7/7): A long-horizon evidence/session/typed-memory stream is source, not an executable. The product horizon triage gate joins three existing keepers — principled stop via KL + quality-peak that wins before cliff or continue (stop-dont-drift / Sun & Guo arXiv:2609.24101), Knowledge Triage that pins constraints via TypeCompact / TypeDecompose and escalates Unsafe below B_min (triage-dont-uniform / Zerhoudi et al. arXiv:2608.22752), and cliff truncate+signature that discards prior cliffs without rewrite or nest (cliff-dont-drift / Nguyen et al. arXiv:2609.26779) — into one measurable final action continue|cliff|stop|unsafe. Blind full-budget + uniform + nested rewrite differs from the gate; principled stop wins over cliff under pressure; below B_min the gate escalates rather than silently dropping a pinned constraint; TypeCompact pins constraints on the continue path where uniform drops them; cliff is faithful while nest is unfaithful; prior cliffs are discarded without nest-growing token mass; the gate covers all four actions on the right fixtures while concurrent blind drifts, and TypeDecompose has zero locality violations while token-chunk violates.

Still a hunch: whether the same three-stage precedence holds on a live long-horizon coding/agent harness (real typed memory stores, real stop evidence streams, real token budgets, real tool traces) rather than the stub kitchen working set and toy session; whether a learned type classifier is needed vs the discrete Type labels; residual propagation C_t ← L_t ← C_{t-1} as a quantitative recall channel beyond discard; and how stop vs unsafe precedence should resolve when both stop evidence and a below-B_min typed budget fire in the same turn on a live harness.
